Privacy Policy

Who's Paying · Last updated: 16 August 2026

Who's Paying is a simple local app. You add names and spin a wheel to pick who pays. This policy describes what the app does and does not collect.

Data we store

Names you type are saved only on your device so they are still there the next time you open the app. They are not uploaded to our servers. We do not create accounts, and we do not ask for your email, phone number, or location.

Data we do not collect

  • No analytics or advertising identifiers
  • No crash reports sent by this app
  • No contacts, photos, or microphone access
  • No sale of personal information

Internet

The app is meant to work offline. The Android package may still declare a normal network permission used by the operating system and store updates. The app itself does not send your names over the network.

Children

The app is a casual utility and is not directed at children under 13. Do not enter names or other information about children if you do not want that information stored on the device.

Your choices

Remove a name in the app to delete it from the wheel. Uninstalling the app deletes locally stored names.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
